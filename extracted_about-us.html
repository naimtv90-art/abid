About Abid Hearing Care Centre (AHCC)

Founded in 2014 by Mohammad Haidar Ali (Audiologist with Clinical Competence in Audiology), Abid Hearing Care Centre has grown to become Dhaka’s premier clinical audiology clinic dedicated to patient-centered, ethical hearing rehabilitation.

Our Mission & Clinical Philosophy

Hearing loss is not simply a volume problem — it affects brain cognitive health, emotional connection, and quality of life. At AHCC, our philosophy is anchored on three clinical pillars:

  • Precision Diagnosis Before Device: We never sell hearing devices over the counter. Every client undergoes rigorous audiometric evaluation in an ISO-calibrated soundproof booth.
  • Certified Technology: As an authorized partner of Swiss Sonova (Phonak) and leading German/Danish brands, we guarantee 100% genuine hardware with international warranty support.
  • Continuous Rehabilitation: Hearing adaptation takes time. We provide free follow-up fine-tuning sessions, regular cleaning, and auditory counselling for the lifetime of your device.

Clinic Organization & Team

Our multidisciplinary team comprises 9 full-time audiology and technical specialists and 12 support staff operating across clinical audiology, acoustic ear-mould fabrication, and specialized device servicing.
