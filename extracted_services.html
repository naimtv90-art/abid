Abid Hearing Care Centre provides a complete spectrum of audiological diagnostic evaluations and technical maintenance services performed with calibrated, state-of-the-art medical instruments.

1. Diagnostic Hearing Evaluations

We perform comprehensive tests to determine the exact type, degree, and configuration of hearing impairment:

  • Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA): Air and bone conduction testing to evaluate hearing thresholds across standard speech and high frequencies.
  • Auditory Brainstem Response (ABR / BERA): Measures electrical brain activity in response to sound; vital for infants and neuro-audiological diagnosis.
  • Auditory Steady-State Response (ASSR): Provides frequency-specific threshold estimates for infants and severe hearing impairments.
  • Otoacoustic Emissions (OAE): Tests cochlear outer hair cell function for rapid newborn hearing screening.
  • Impedance Audiometry & Tympanometry: Evaluates middle ear function, eardrum mobility, and Eustachian tube dysfunction.

2. Digital Hearing Aid Selection & Computerized Fitting

Using digital manufacturer fitting software, our audiologists calibrate hearing devices specifically to match your prescription curve. We ensure optimal speech clarity while protecting residual hearing against loud sounds.

3. Custom Ear Mould & Acoustic Shell Laboratory

In-house laboratory producing medical-grade ear moulds customized to the unique ear canal anatomy of every patient:

  • Soft silicone moulds for pediatric comfort and feedback-free high-power amplification.
  • Hard acrylic moulds for durability and easy insertion for adult patients.
  • Custom micro-shells for invisible Completely-in-the-Canal (CIC) and In-the-Canal (ITC) instruments.

4. Maintenance, Repair & Sonic Cleaning

Complete servicing center equipped to repair and maintain all major hearing aid models:

  • Speaker/Receiver replacement & sound distortion correction.
  • Microphone mesh unclogging and transducer replacement.
  • Ultrasonic moisture extraction and wax barrier clearing.
  • Battery door replacement and contacts cleaning.
