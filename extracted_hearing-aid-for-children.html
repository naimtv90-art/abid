Pediatric Hearing Care: Nurturing Speech & Language

Hearing is the biological cornerstone of spoken language, social learning, and intellectual development. A child learns speech by listening to their parents and surroundings from the earliest months of infancy.

Why Early Pediatric Intervention Matters

If hearing loss is left undiagnosed in infants, speech milestones are severely delayed. With early diagnosis via newborn OAE/BERA screening and timely fitting of pediatric hearing devices (such as Phonak Sky), children can learn, attend mainstream schools, and communicate with complete confidence.

Key Features of Pediatric Hearing Aids

  • Tamperproof Battery Drawers: Prevents curious infants and toddlers from opening the battery compartment.
  • Robust Water & Dust Resistance: Rated IP68 to withstand play, sweat, and unpredictable outdoor conditions.
  • Visual Indicator Lights: Reassures parents and teachers that the device is powered and functioning.
  • Roger Wireless FM Compatibility: Direct streaming from the classroom teacher’s wireless microphone straight to the child’s hearing aids.
